Our Language Learning Adventure Rules

🍰 Rule #1: Consistency over Intensity. Studying 10 minutes every day while sipping tea is better than cramming for 3 hours once a month.

🧁 Rule #2: Embrace Mistakes. Making mistakes is just the flour mess before baking a delicious cake!

🍵 Rule #3: Speak Out Loud. Say every word out loud as if ordering at a cute bakery counter.

Understanding Chinese Characters Basics

Chinese characters (Hanzi) are built like recipes using basic components called radicals.

Learn radicals first! For example, 氵 signifies water (found in 河 river, 酒 alcohol), while 讠 signifies speech (found in 话 speak, 语 language).

4

Pronunciation Guide for Japanese Hiragana/Katakana

Japanese vowels are always clean and short: A (ah), I (ee), U (oo), E (eh), O (oh).

Use Hiragana for native Japanese words and Katakana for foreign borrowed words (e.g. ケーキ - Kēki for Cake!).

5

Korean Hangul Essentials

Hangul was scientifically designed by King Sejong in 1443 to be learned in a single afternoon!

Letters are stacked into syllable blocks (Consonant + Vowel + Optional Final Consonant). Example: 빵 (Bbang = Bread).

6

Tagalog Grammar Quick Start

Tagalog often places the verb first in a sentence (Verb - Subject - Object pattern).

Add politeness markers: Always append Po and Opo when speaking to elders or customer service staff!

7

Recommended Learning Schedule

Mon / Wed / Fri: 15 mins Vocabulary & Flashcards on Anki / LingoDeer.

Tue / Thu: 15 mins Worksheets & Writing practice in your notebook.

Weekend: Watch a drama/movie or listen to songs in your target language with green tea!
