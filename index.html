Rules, Schedules & Video Masterclasses

Interactive learning guides, daily study schedules, and your recommended YouTube video lessons!

🍰 Our Language Learning Adventure Rules

• Consistency over Intensity: Studying 10 minutes every day while sipping tea is better than cramming once a month!

• Embrace Mistakes: Making mistakes is just the flour mess before baking a delicious cake!

• Speak Out Loud: Say every word out loud as if ordering at a cute bakery counter.

📅 Recommended Weekly Learning Schedule

• Mon / Wed / Fri: 15 mins Vocabulary & Flashcards on Quizlet / LingoDeer.

• Tue / Thu: 15 mins Worksheets & Writing practice in your notebook.

• Weekend: Watch a video lesson below or listen to songs in your target language!
